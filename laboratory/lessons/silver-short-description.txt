Source-based equilibrium diagrams. Animations are illustrative, not calculated reaction rates. Chloride precipitation, copper cementation and the fresh-solution plating cell are separate examples. The cell assumes ideal free-ion activities of 0.010 for Cu2+ and Ag+ at 25 C; no kinetic overpotential is calculated. Platinum supports silver deposition but does not change the Ag+/Ag equilibrium potential after a silver phase is established.
